Symptom
Every fragment placed, every caption written — efficient, and nobody would keep it.
Looked out
Read user reviews of AIGC photo apps; studied real collages people make in 醒图 Retouchpic.
Diagnosis
I optimized for efficiency and over-trusted AI's taste.
People don't want an AI's day. They want their day, made beautiful.
  • Keep-original is the default
  • AI is opt-in, per photo, voice and text
  • ≤ 2 art pieces per page; people never redrawn
  • Hand-made texture: tape, pencil marks, stickers
PRINCIPLEAI proposes. The author decides.
